Why it looks like this

form and function, one shape

The read

Nine out of ten strangers have to name it “heater” in a second. That job is carried by the recessed three-bar grille, the two control knobs, the nameplate and the tall, slightly leaning stance — not by paint. The look is a Goal-1 promise, so it wins ties against aero.

The air

The nose bows 200 mm so the flat face never slams the wind, and the tail draws down to a Ø700 pod so the wake stays small. The driver reclines feet-forward with eyes 670 mm below the roofline — the orange bottle on the back of the heater. The driver is the fuel.

The rules

Gravity and a push, nothing stored. Steel spine, shutterply deck, welded roll hoop over the helmet line, harness always. Every number on this page passed the envelope, mass and rollover checks before anything gets cut.
